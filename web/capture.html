1 · The object

Funerary and sacred material defaults to source-community disclosure. A mesh is a replication asset, and for such material visibility may itself be the harm.

2 · What is in shot

Without a scale reference the model has shape but no size — and an object with no size cannot be measured, compared against a written description, or matched to an excavation record. It is the single highest-value thing to put in the frame.

5 · Session

Export session gives you a .zip containing the photographs, the measurements and a checksum for each image. The photographs are the archive — the 3D model is derived from them and can be rebuilt later with better software, but the photographs cannot be recreated from the model. Do this before you leave the site, and open the file to check it.
